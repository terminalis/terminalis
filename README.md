I develop software primarily for my own use and publish it for anyone else who may find it useful.

**What to expect**

- Your data stays on your device unless you send it somewhere, and it can always be exported.
- No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking.
- No prompts, upsells or required accounts.
- The core of every app is free and open source, and always will be. Paid features may come later, for extras or for cloud hosting that carries a running cost. Features that are free will remain free.

**Issues and feature requests**

Bug reports are always welcome, as are reasonable feature requests. As these apps are built primarily for my own use, requests will be considered but cannot be guaranteed.

Releases are published on GitHub. Linux desktop apps are also available from a signed Flatpak repository, and Android apps from a signed F-Droid repository, both at [terminalis.github.io](https://terminalis.github.io/).
