### echo

I develop software primarily for my own use and publish it for anyone else who finds it useful.

The aim is software that keeps your work safe and your data portable, stays out of your way, and improves with each release.

**Principles**

- **Ownership.** Your data is stored on your device and leaves it only at your direction. It can always be exported.
- **Privacy.** These apps contain no analytics, telemetry or usage tracking.
- **Focus.** No prompts, upsells or mandatory sign-ups stand between you and the purpose of the app.
- **Open source.** Every app I publish is free and open source. A paid feature will be offered only where it carries a genuine running cost and either improves the app or answers a request from its users. Features that are free will remain free.

**Issues and feature requests**

Bug reports are always welcome, as are reasonable feature requests. As these apps are built primarily for my own use, requests will be considered but cannot be guaranteed.

Releases are published on GitHub, and Linux desktop apps are also available from a signed repository, [terminalis/packages](https://github.com/terminalis/packages).
